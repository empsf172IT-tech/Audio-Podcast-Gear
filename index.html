Warranty & Support

Register your SONAVEX equipment to activate your extended warranty, access premium support, and stay updated on firmware releases.

How long is the warranty period?

All microphones and interfaces come with a standard 2-year warranty. Headphones include a 1-year warranty.

How do I find my serial number?

The 12-digit serial number is located on the bottom of your device or on the original packaging barcode label.

What is covered under warranty?

Manufacturer defects and hardware failures are fully covered. Accidental damage or cosmetic wear is not covered.
